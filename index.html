BuildWrite Apps

Three small apps for running building jobs from your phone. One account works in all three, and your team's jobs are private to your team.

Job BookA running to-do list for every job, with permits and the job's status. Start here.

Getting started

  1. Open Job Book and tap Create an account instead. Use the email address your invite was sent for, and type a password twice.
  2. Name your team — your business name, or your own. You start with an empty job book.
  3. Sign in to Receipt Run and Field Measure with the same email and password. Jobs you add in any of them show up in the others.
  4. Put each app on your home screen so it opens like a normal app (below).
These apps are invite-only for now. If Job Book says there's no invite for your email, ask the person who shared this page.

Add to your home screen

iPhone (Safari): open an app, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): open an app, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
Do it once for each of the three apps.
Something not working, or an idea? Every app has a Feedback button at the top — it comes straight to us.
